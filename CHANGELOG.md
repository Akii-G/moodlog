# Changelog

Sottovoce for the PC, Sottovoce for iPhone and Sottovoce for Android have their own version numbers. Each GitHub release is named after the PC version and includes all three apps.

## Sottovoce for the PC

### 3.4.0 (2026-11-26)

- Nights without your watch: in Report for your doctor, Short nights lists the nights under 4 hours. Delete the ones when your watch was charging: they go away from Sottovoce on the PC and on your phones, and Health won't bring them back with the next sync.
- One medicine, several names: if you logged a medicine in Apple Health and then in Dose under another name, Medicine names in the report window gives it one name, on the PC and on your phones. Sottovoce suggests the names that look the same; a dose marked in both apps on the same day counts once.

### 3.3.1 (2026-11-25)

- Medication from Dose: a medicine taken in different amounts at different times (for example 1 in the morning and ½ in the evening) shows the right amount for each dose.

### 3.3.0 (2026-11-25)

- Dose, the sister app for your medicines: if Dose is on this PC, its doses show on the day and in the doctor report, and starting, changing or stopping a medicine in Dose adds a Medication change event here, once. Turn the events off in Customize.
- Doctor report: for each medicine, how many doses were taken, skipped, not taken and still to take today; medicines you no longer take are listed apart, under Previous medication. On the day, a dose nobody marked now says Not taken, or Yet to take before its time.

### 3.2.0 (2026-11-25)

- Report for your doctor: two new periods, the last 7 days and the last 2 weeks, handy before a check-up.

### 3.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- Activities: next to the feeling words, optional tags for what you did — Work, Walk, Friends, Rest and more. They show on the day, sync with the phones, can be searched, and the doctor report lists the ones you logged most often. Turn them off in Customize.
- Your diary as a book: Export → Your diary as a book (.pdf). Your notes month by month, with the mood faces, feeling words, activities and events, laid out as a PDF to keep or print. Private moments stay out unless you choose to include them.

### 3.0.2 (2026-11-24)

- Before the appointment: in Events, set the date of your next appointment with your doctor or therapist. In the week before, the Log shows a calm recap of how things have gone since the last one, and you can keep a list of things you want to talk about. Tick them once said; the rest can go in the doctor report if you choose. Synced with your phones.
- Your week, gently: on Sunday evening and on Monday, a few calm words about your week on the Log — days logged, the usual mood, your best day. No scores; turn it off in Customize.

### 3.0.1 (2026-11-23)

- Search in Notes now also finds feeling words and events, ignores capitals and accents (“perche” finds “Perché”), and shows what it found in bold. Clicking an event opens it.

### 3.0.0 (2026-11-22)

- Moodlog is now Sottovoce — a quiet mood diary: a new name and a softer icon. Your data, settings and phone sync stay exactly as they were.
- Default names for backups, exports and reports now start with sottovoce-. On Linux the package is now called sottovoce and replaces moodlog; the moodlog command still works.

### 2.12.2 (2026-11-22)

- Windows: the round window buttons of the Tahoe style no longer stretch into tall bars.

### 2.12.1 (2026-11-22)

- Feeling words: the ones you pick now light up in the Tahoe style too.
- Reminder, kind word and the Gridgets link are now in ☰ → Customize your experience, and they apply at once.

### 2.12.0 (2026-11-21)

- Sottovoce for Windows 11 and 10: the same app, with phone sync, the daily reminder as a Windows notification, and an installer. Your data is in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sottovoce.

### 2.11.0 (2026-11-21)

- Feeling words: next to a mood you can pick words like Joy, Tiredness or Worry, more precise than five faces. They show on the day, sync with the phones, and the report lists the ones you picked most often.
- Writing prompts: an empty note shows a gentle question, a different one each day, with a button for another. Never required. Both can be turned off in Customize.

### 2.10.0 (2026-11-21)

- Questionnaires for your doctor (☰ menu): PHQ-9 about low mood and GAD-7 about anxiety, with the official words in English and Italian. Sottovoce shows only your score, a chart of scores over time, and the report can include them. Two weeks after the last one, a small card on the Log page asks if you'd like to answer again.
- Charts: "Most common" now counts every entry, like the chart of how often you logged each mood and like the phones. Before, it counted each day's average, so a tie between days could show a different mood from the chart.

### 2.9.0 (2026-11-21)

- Private notes and dreams: tick Private on a note and it never goes in the doctor report; a private dream is only counted there, never shown. In Customize you can hide the tick, or make new ones start private.
- Customize your experience (☰ menu, the tour, and once after updating): turn off Dreams or Medication, and choose whether days with several moods show all of them in a grid or only the latest.

### 2.8.1 (2026-11-21)

- Medication: a Medication button on each day opens the list of that day's doses, each with a pill in the colour of its status, the time, the amount and Taken or Skipped.

### 2.8.0 (2026-11-20)

- Medication from Apple Health: with Sottovoce for iPhone 1.6, the medications you log in the Health app and the doses you took or skipped arrive with the health data. They show on each day, and the report for your doctor has a Medication section.

### 2.7.0 (2026-11-20)

- A daily reminder (☰ → Reminder and kind word): one quiet desktop notification at the time you choose, only on days you haven't logged a mood yet.
- A kind word on hard days: after a few very hard days close together, the Log shows a short, kind note with a helpline. It never tells anyone, and you can hide it or turn it off.

### 2.6.1 (2026-11-20)

- Sottovoce for Android 1.1.0 is out: a new look like the iPhone app, a taller calendar, and it finds this PC more reliably when pop-os.local doesn't work on the phone. Nothing changes on the PC.

### 2.6.0 (2026-11-19)

- Sottovoce for Android: a new app for Android phones that syncs moods, energy, anxiety, notes, events and dreams with this PC, encrypted, and sends sleep, steps and resting heart rate from Health Connect. Phone sync has the steps to set it up.

### 2.5.1 (2026-11-19)

- A privacy policy: ☰ menu → Privacy shows in short what Sottovoce does with your data (nothing leaves your devices except the encrypted phone sync), with a link to the full policy.
- The phone sync receiver no longer writes the address of a request to the system log in full, so a token sent in the address can't end up there.

### 2.5.0 (2026-11-19)

- Encrypted phone sync: Sottovoce for iPhone 1.4 encrypts everything it sends with your token, and the token itself never crosses the network. Phone sync shows whether the last connection was encrypted, and has a new Change token button.

### 2.4.0 (2026-11-19)

- A welcome tour for new users: a few friendly, animated steps that point at each part of the window and explain it. It opens by itself on a new install; see it any time from the ☰ menu → Show the tour.

### 2.3.2 (2026-11-19)

- Doctor report: “Also what the dreams were about” can always be ticked, and ticking it includes dreams too. Before, it stayed greyed out until “Include dreams” was ticked, so it was easy to miss.

### 2.3.1 (2026-11-19)

- After an update, the phone sync receiver switches to the new version by itself. Before, it kept running the old one until it was restarted, so new things such as dreams did not sync.

### 2.3.0 (2026-11-19)

- Dream journal: a new Dreams tab. Write down a dream with how it felt and tags such as nightmare or vivid, or note that you didn't remember one. Dreams show as a moon in the calendar, with a summary in the tab and in Charts, and sync with the iPhone.
- The doctor report can include dreams: off by default, then just how many and the nightmare nights, or also what they were about.
- Tabs take less room, so the window fits in half a screen again.

### 2.2.0 (2026-11-19)

- Sottovoce speaks Italian. It follows your system language, or choose one in the ☰ menu → Language. Dates, charts, the doctor report and exports are translated too.

### 2.1.0 (2026-11-19)

- A “What's new” window opens once after each update, with the full changelog. Open it again any time from the ☰ menu.

### 2.0.1 (2026-11-19)

- Tahoe style: the selected Energy and Anxiety numbers were invisible (white on white). They are blue again, in light and dark.
- Screenshots of the app are now in the README.

### 2.0.0 (2026-11-19)

- Sottovoce for iPhone: a new app that syncs moods, energy, anxiety, notes and events with Sottovoce on your PC over your home Wi-Fi.
- Every entry and event now has an ID and a change time: the newest change wins, deletions sync too, and nothing is overwritten when two devices save at once.
- Saving merges changes written meanwhile by the phone or another computer.
- Your existing data is upgraded automatically.

### 1.9.1 (2026-11-19)

- Tahoe style: the top bar, tabs and buttons now change too when a system theme is installed in ~/.config/gtk-4.0.

### 1.9.0 (2026-11-19)

- Two styles in the new ☰ menu: Default (GNOME) and Tahoe (macOS-like, with frosted cards, pill buttons and red/yellow/green window buttons).

### 1.8.0 (2026-11-18)

- Energy and anxiety: two optional 1–5 scales on every entry, with their own chart.
- Report for your doctor: a printable PDF for a chosen period, with charts, events and a day-by-day table. Notes are only included if you ask.
- Backup and restore as one .zip file; restoring saves your current data first.
- Data folder and sync: keep your data in a Syncthing or Nextcloud folder to use Sottovoce on two computers, with merging of conflict copies.

### 1.7.1 (2026-11-18)

- Fixed sleep showing 24 hours every night when the shortcut sent dates without a time.

### 1.7.0 (2026-11-18)

- Export to a spreadsheet (.xlsx) with coloured moods, a summary sheet and charts.

### 1.6.0 (2026-11-18)

- Export everything to a CSV file, one row per day.

- Events: mark things like a medication change. They show as a flag in the calendar and a line on the charts, with your mood in the two weeks before and after.

### 1.4.0 (2026-11-17)

- Days with several moods show a small grid of tiles in the calendar, one per entry.

### 1.3.0 (2026-11-14)

- Several moods a day, each with its own time.
- New chart: average mood by time of day.

### 1.2.0 (2026-11-14)

- Link with the mood widget of the Gridgets GNOME extension.

### 1.1.0 (2026-11-13)

- Phone sync: an iPhone Shortcut sends sleep, steps and heart rate to Sottovoce.
- New chart comparing your mood with your health data.

### 1.0.0 (2026-11-13)

- First version: a mood and a note each day, a month calendar, charts and a notes list, all saved in a plain CSV file.
